title: Welcome
---

Broadway is a historical archive of American culture. This digital exhibition reads musicals as evidence—of what a generation feared, celebrated, argued over, and imagined could be different.

I came to that question through a lifetime in the theatre. The galleries, catalog, and thematic essays are the research. The photographs under “I was there” are how the research started: nights at productions I actually attended.

This is not a collection of reviews. Each exhibition card asks what a production did onstage, what historical moment produced it, and why it still matters. The personal journey is the origin story. The thesis is larger: together, these musicals tell a story of America.
